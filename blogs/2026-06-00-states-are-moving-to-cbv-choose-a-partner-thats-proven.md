President Trump's H.R. 1 legislation mandates that states implement Medicaid work requirements by January 1, 2027, requiring non-exempt adults aged 19-64 to document at least 80 hours per month of qualifying work, forcing states to modernize benefits verification using consent-based verification technology. The article argues that most legacy verification systems cannot handle the new compliance requirements, which include checks at application, redetermination, and every six months thereafter. Argyle positions itself as a proven implementation partner, having built consent-based verification infrastructure in the mortgage industry before expanding to government benefits programs.
