Argyle announced enhancements to its government verification solution for state agencies managing Medicaid, SNAP, and TANF eligibility, addressing new requirements from H.R. 1 (the One Big Beautiful Bill Act) that mandate Medicaid work requirements and more frequent payroll-driven verification by January 1, 2027. New capabilities include flexible data sources, agency-ready reporting, one-click reauthentication, and enhanced employer search. Argyle reports being active in over 10 states and supporting major benefits administration providers.
